Phase 14: what sending to a viewer costs the service
====================================================

Every WebSocket frame the live server sends is now a scope on the service's
own timeline, named for what the frame carries, with the frame's size on a
value lane. Measured 2026-09-05 on the dev box (32 cores), headless Chrome
as the one viewer, target orbit-e2e-box3d --threads 3, 5 s capture, sampling
at 1 kHz with DWARF unwinding and context switches, packed wire.

Live tab, service rows, whole capture (screenshot v-send-scopes.png):

  scope                 count   total       avg         min         max
  capture pass            720   3.831 s     5.321 ms    5.184 ms    11.217 ms
  read samples            721   100.288 ms  139.095 us  76.223 us   350.391 us
  drain scope rings       720   38.720 ms   53.778 us   21.775 us   139.179 us
  read context switches   721   15.599 ms   21.635 us   3.076 us    167.382 us
  unwind                11509   13.193 ms   1.146 us    151 ns      56.376 us
  push to viewer          721   11.923 ms   16.536 us   7.165 us    61.116 us
  encode events           721   9.648 ms    13.380 us   2.297 us    38.812 us
  ws send events          721   7.677 ms    10.648 us   1.413 us    53.840 us
  symbolize             11509   6.991 ms    607 ns      58 ns       44.760 us
  ws send string          165   1.740 ms    10.544 us   572 ns      54.727 us
  ws send name             36   37.328 us   1.036 us    592 ns      10.612 us

Reading: one event batch per 5 ms capture pass; encoding it (packed) costs
13 us and handing it to the socket 11 us, so a viewer costs the service
about 25 us per pass, 0.5% of the pass, at 398 KB/s on the wire. The capture
pass itself is the 5 ms sleep between polls; the work inside it is the
170 us of read samples + drain + context switches. Interned strings go out
once each (165 over the capture) at the same 10 us as an event batch.

Not measured: more than one viewer, the deflate wire, a slow network where
the sink's send would block (the scope would show it).
