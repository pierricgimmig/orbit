Phase 15: a render pass with the self profile, and what it found
================================================================

Measured 2026-09-05 on the dev box, headless Chrome (SwiftShader, so the
browser's own frame period is 120-170 ms and says nothing; the viewer's
CPU phases are what is compared), Box3D --threads 3, a 4 s capture with
symbols, sampling at 1 kHz, DWARF. Self pane open throughout, which adds
its own timeline (SelfPane ~0.35 ms) to every frame. Script:
scratch measure2.py (the e2e harness's Chrome + Service, __orbit_self).

New instrumentation this pass: frame_period_us / outside_frame_us (egui's
dt against the outermost Frame scope: everything eframe, WebGPU submit and
the compositor cost, invisible to the viewer's scopes), gpu_prepare_us /
gpu_paint_us (CPU time inside the egui-wgpu callbacks, read one frame
late), SelfTimeline / SelfPane / ReportPanel scopes.

Before (commit d61a44bb8):

  state                    Frame    listing/frame  listing avg  ReportPanel
  fit, idle                4221 us  1.3            1037 us      (inside Frame, unscoped: ~0.8 ms)
  zoomed x6, pointer still 3271 us  0.6            1109 us
  zoomed, pointer moving   2541 us  0.3            1109 us
  panning (D held)         2439 us  0.2            1130 us
  live capture, follow     2596 us  0.1            1130 us

Three findings, three fixes:

1. The follow ease never lands. `t0 += (target - t0) * k` converges but
   never reaches the target in integer nanoseconds: 234, 121, 61, 32, 16,
   9, 4 ns a frame for a second after the live edge stops, and every one
   of those is a new window, a full listing of 20,000 primitives and a
   full upload of a still picture. Fix: snap when both edges are within
   an eighth of a pixel. Idle listing per frame: 1.3 -> 0.2.

2. The listing hashed every instance's name string every frame:
   `color_for` did an intern lookup (SipHash) plus an FNV over the name
   bytes per instance, a third of the walk. The intern table now stores
   the name hash at insert and the id map uses a one-multiply hasher.
   Listing of 15,000 primitives: 1109 -> 734 us (-34%).

3. The sampling report's egui grid: 200 rows x 5 widgets laid out every
   frame, 0.81 ms, forty percent of an idle frame, whatever the panel
   showed. The Flat report is now laid out by hand like the Functions
   view, only the rows in the clip rect, every row listed (no 200 cap).
   ReportPanel: 810 -> 190 us. The sort comparators also stopped
   allocating a lowercase copy per comparison.

After (this commit):

  state                    Frame    ReportPanel  listing avg  SelfPane
  fit, idle                1383 us  203 us       258 us       358 us
  zoomed x6, pointer still 1503 us  190 us       841 us       337 us
  zoomed, pointer moving   1318 us  182 us       --           336 us
  panning (D held)         1330 us  180 us       878 us       340 us
  live capture, follow     1588 us  175 us       --           355 us

Frame: 4.2 -> 1.4 ms idle, 3.3 -> 1.5 ms zoomed, 2.6 -> 1.6 ms live. A
third of what remains is the Self pane itself, which a user never has
open; without it the viewer's CPU frame is about 1 ms.

Also in this pass: sampled frames are no longer laid out on the thread
tracks (commit d61a44bb8), which took the Box3D capture from ~200,000
primitives to ~15,000 zoomed in and 58 at fit.

Still on the table, by size:
- PrimitiveListing when it runs: 0.8 ms for 15k instances, of which
  PoolDispatch (worker hand-off, walk and join) is most; the ListingCache
  reuses lanes only when the window is unchanged, so a pan re-walks
  every lane. A per-lane cache keyed on the lane's version with the
  x-transform applied at upload (a uniform, not a re-walk) would make a
  pan free.
- Rasterize 0.18-0.36 ms per pixel-LOD frame (RasterWalk + ToRgba8 +
  RemapTheme); the theme remap could be folded into the walk.
- Chrome 0.16 ms: the transport and capture strip, laid out every frame.
- gpu_prepare 0.1-0.36 ms: the instance buffer write, proportional to
  the instances uploaded; a Flags-only change re-packs the whole buffer.
- The browser side (outside_frame_us) is unmeasurable headless; on a real
  GPU it is the egui tessellation and the WebGPU submit. Worth a look
  with the browser's own profiler on the user's machine.
